Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Short version: our extensions don't collect your data, and this website doesn't track you.

Our extensions

Our extensions have no analytics, telemetry, advertising or accounts. Settings and logs are stored in your browser. Each extension's full privacy policy is here:

  • Tab Rotator privacy policy: covers the license check and the optional config and alert features.
  • Regex Find privacy policy.

Purchases

When you buy a license, Lemon Squeezy processes the order as merchant of record and shares with us what we need to support you: your name, email address, what you bought, and the license key. We use these only to provide and support your license, and keep them as long as needed for that and for tax records. Payment card details go to Lemon Squeezy and its payment processors, never to us. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Emails

If you email us, we keep the conversation to help you, and never add you to a mailing list without asking.
